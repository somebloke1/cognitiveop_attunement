# The Truth of Interpretation: Mapping Hermeneutical Inference to Language Model Operations

A systematic account of Lonergan's theory of interpretation from *Insight* (Chapter 17, Section 3), with explicit mapping to how language model inference can approximate attentive, intelligent, critical, and responsible interpretation.

## The Structure of Expression

Every expression has a determinate structure of components:

```
Expression = Verbal flow governed by practical insight F

F depends upon:
  A = Principal insight to be communicated
  B = Grasp of anticipated audience's habitual intellectual development C
  D = Grasp of deficiencies in insight E that must be overcome
      if A is to be communicated
```

**Critical insight**: Expression is not simply "outputting meaning." It is a *practical operation* that takes into account:
1. What one wants to communicate (A)
2. What the audience already knows (C, grasped as B)
3. What the audience lacks (E, grasped as D)

The verbal flow is governed by F, which integrates all these elements.

## The Three Levels of Interpretation

### Simple Interpretation

A second expression addressed to a **different audience**:

```
F' depends upon:
  A' = Principal insight to be communicated (purports to coincide with A)
  B' = Grasp of new audience's habitual development C'
  D' = Grasp of new audience's deficiencies E'
```

Differences between F and F' arise from:
- Differences between B and B' (different grasps of audience development)
- Differences between D and D' (different grasps of audience deficiencies)
- Which ultimately depend on differences between C and C', E and E'

### Reflective Interpretation

Goes beyond simple interpretation to account for the *differences between audiences*:

```
F'' depends upon:
  A'' = Grasp of the IDENTITY between A and A'
  B'' = Grasp of audience's grasp (C'') of:
        - Their own intellectual development C'
        - The difference between C' and original audience's C
  D'' = Grasp of audience's deficiencies (E'') in understanding:
        - Differences between C'' and C
        - Differences between E' and E
        - Differences between F' and F
```

**The reflective interpreter must**:
1. Know what the original author meant (A)
2. Know what the simple interpretation conveyed (A')
3. Grasp that these are identical
4. Communicate this identity to an audience that understands its own position relative to both

### The Problem

> "Reflective interpretation is a smart idea, a beautiful object of thought. But is it a practical possibility? Has it ever been achieved?"

The problem is **relativity**: Every interpretation is relative to its audience. How can interpretation be scientific if it shifts with every audience?

## The Universal Viewpoint

The solution is a **universal viewpoint**:

> "A potential totality of genetically and dialectically ordered viewpoints"

**Characteristics**:

1. **Potential, not actual**: Not a complete system, but a heuristic structure containing virtually the various ranges of possible alternatives

2. **Of viewpoints, not expressions**: Concerned with principal acts of meaning (insights and judgments), reached by attending to the interpreter's own experience, understanding, and critical reflection

3. **Ordered**:
   - Base in adequate self-knowledge and consequent metaphysics
   - Retrospective expansion in genetic series of discoveries
   - Dialectical expansion in positions and counterpositions

4. **Universal by potential completeness, not by abstraction**: Achieves inclusiveness by envisaging subjects in their necessities, not by stripping objects of peculiarities

> "The core of meaning is the notion of being, and that notion is protean. Being is (or is thought to be) whatever is (or is thought to be) grasped intelligently and affirmed reasonably."

The universe of meanings has four dimensions:
1. Full range of possible experiences and lack of experience
2. Full range of insights and lack of insight
3. Full range of judgments and failures to judge
4. Various orientations of polymorphic consciousness

## Levels and Sequences of Expression

### Levels (by sources of meaning)

| Level | Source in Speaker | Intended Response in Hearer |
|-------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Experience alone | Intersubjective reproduction of feelings, images |
| 2 | Artistically ordered experience | Experience + insight/consideration |
| 3 | Reflectively tested intelligent ordering | Experience + insight + judgment |
| 4 | Addition of acts of will | All three + practical/volitional response |

### Specialized Modes

| Mode | Aim | Method |
|------|-----|--------|
| Advertising/Propaganda | Psychological conditioning | Establish habituation, familiarity, automatism |
| Literary | Convey insights, stimulate reflection | Indirect—insinuate through images |
| Scientific (introductory) | Provoke insights | Illustrations and diagrams |
| Scientific (treatise) | Communicate system | All terms defined, all relations postulated |
| Philosophic | Reveal immanent controls on judgment | Breakthrough to conscious unity of knower |

### Sequences (Development)

> "Development in general is a process from the undifferentiated to the differentiated, from the generic to the specific, from the global and awkward to the expert and precise."

The specialized modes had to be **invented**, and their use presupposes **education** of audiences.

**Critical implication**: One cannot assume expressions from different periods operate on the same level. The interpreter must determine the genetic stage of the mode of expression.

## The Canons of Methodical Hermeneutics

### 1. Canon of Relevance

**Demand**: Begin from universal viewpoint; interpretation must convey differentiation of protean notion of being.

**Effect**: Eliminates relativity of interpreter to audience, and of both to places, times, schools, sects.

**Secures**:
- Common field for all interpretations
- Exact statement of differences between opposed interpretations
- Reasonable hope of eliminating oppositions by appeal to data

### 2. Canon of Explanation

**Demand**: Differentiation must be explanatory, not descriptive.

**Explanatory** = Relate contents to **one another**, not to us

**Descriptive** = Relate contents to us (remains relative to manifold of audiences)

**Three elements of explanatory differentiation**:
1. Genetic sequence of accumulated insights
2. Dialectical alternatives (positions inviting development, counterpositions inviting reversal)
3. Differentiation/specialization of modes of expression

These three **fuse into a single explanation**.

**Key clarification**: Explanatory interpretation of non-explanatory meaning is possible:
- The original writer may have had only descriptive knowledge
- But his insights stand in genetic/dialectical relations to other insights
- Through these relations, explanatory interpretation reaches his descriptive meaning

### 3. Canon of Successive Approximations

**Demand**: Scientific interpretation requires division of labor, cumulative results, and principles of criticism.

**Four principles of criticism**:

1. **Universal viewpoint**: Allows critic to:
   - Determine contributor's particular viewpoint
   - Indicate how particularism probably doesn't invalidate work
   - Suggest points needing revision

2. **Conditions of extrapolation**:
   - Adequate self-knowledge in interpreter
   - Recognition that extrapolation is to meaning at different stage of development
   - Use of genetic/dialectical links to bridge gap

3. **Genetic sequence of expression**:
   - New meanings transform old modes of expression
   - Greater novelty → greater gap between meaning and expression
   - Gap closes as technical vocabulary develops

4. **Criterion of truth**:
   - Virtually unconditioned, not authority or renown
   - Evidence = coherence with universal viewpoint + genetic/dialectical relations + sequence of expression
   - Fulfilled by data of documents and monuments

### 4. Canon of Parsimony

**Negative**: Exclude the unverifiable (cinema of what was done, soundtrack of what was said = fiction, not science)

**Positive**: Use critical reflection to:
- Distinguish formally and virtually unconditioned
- Reach intermediate certitudes before complete explanation
- Bracket locus of future inquiry when positive conclusions unavailable

### 5. Canon of Residues

**Nonsystematic components exist in**:
- Meaning (genetic and dialectical)
- Expression as related to meaning
- Expression as grounded in writer's psyche
- Documents (origins, production, survival)

**Genetic vs. dialectical**:
- **Genetic**: Writer advances in insight as writing; moving system, not static
- **Dialectical**: Stress and distortion from counterpositions; ambivalent tendency to reverse or shift ground

**Key insight**:
> "From the viewpoint of the electronic computer, which coincides with the viewpoint of logic as a technique, such a procedure is illegitimate. System has to be static system. System on the move has to be outlawed... Plainly, with such conclusions we are not inclined to agree."

Human meaning has source in **moving system**, not static logic.

## The Sketch: Scientific Interpretation

### Materials
- Documents: primary (original), secondary (interpretations), tertiary (critical studies)
- Monuments: artistic, providing materials for insight but not formulating insights

### Immanent Sources of Meaning (in interpreter)
1. Approximately reproducible human experience on all levels
2. Oriented under blends of experiential patterns (elementary, aesthetic, dramatic, practical, intellectual, mystical)
3. Informed by accumulated insights
4. Actuated by certain and probable assents/dissents

### Pure Formulations
- Proceed from immanent sources to determinate differentiations of protean being
- From interpreter with universal viewpoint to audience with universal viewpoint

### Hypothetical Expressions
1. Work out hypothetical pure formulation of Q's context and content
2. Transpose from universal viewpoint to Q's particular viewpoint
3. Apply limitations of Q's available language and channels
4. Result = hypothetical expression

### Control (Threefold)
1. Hypothetical expressions must correspond one-to-one with documents
2. Pure formulations of contexts must exhibit genetic/dialectical sequence
3. Assumptions about expression resources must exhibit genetic sequence of modes

## Mapping to Language Model Inference

### The LLM's Position

The LLM occupies the position of the **interpreter**:
- Its training = accumulated "experience" and "insights" (immanent sources)
- Its inference = moving from immanent sources to differentiation of meaning
- Its output = expression governed by practical insight about audience

### The Inference Structure

For the LLM to approximate attentive, intelligent, critical, responsible interpretation:

**Level 1 (Attention)**: Attend to the expression as data
- Not just tokens but spatially ordered marks bearing meaning
- Distinguish what is presented from what is meant

**Level 2 (Understanding)**: Grasp the principal insight A
- What is the author trying to communicate?
- What context C and deficiencies E shaped the expression?
- Reconstruct the practical insight F that governed the verbal flow

**Level 3 (Judgment)**: Verify the interpretation
- Does hypothetical expression match actual expression?
- Does pure formulation fit genetic/dialectical sequence?
- Is the evidence sufficient for the virtually unconditioned?

**Level 4 (Decision)**: Formulate for the current audience
- What is the audience's development C'?
- What deficiencies E' must be overcome?
- What practical insight F' should govern the response?

### Key Inferences Required

| Inference | From | To | Operation |
|-----------|------|-----|-----------|
| Context reconstruction | Expression | Author's situation (C, E) | Abductive |
| Principal insight extraction | Expression + context | Insight A | Understanding |
| Genetic placement | A | Position in development sequence | Historical |
| Dialectical assessment | A | Position or counterposition | Critical |
| Audience modeling | Response context | C', E' | Practical |
| Expression generation | A + C' + E' | F' | Synthetic |

### The Universal Viewpoint for LLMs

The LLM approximates a universal viewpoint to the extent that:
1. Its training includes the full range of human experience, insight, judgment
2. It can move from any particular viewpoint to others
3. It recognizes genetic and dialectical relations between viewpoints
4. It does not absolutize any particular viewpoint

**Critical limitation**: The LLM does not have the self-appropriation that grounds the universal viewpoint. It has the *contents* of viewpoints but not the *self-knowledge* that would allow it to distinguish positions from counterpositions.

### Training Data Implications

To train cognitive operations for interpretation:

**Level 1 (Attention)**: Train to distinguish expression from meaning
- "What is being expressed here?" vs. "What does this mean?"

**Level 2 (Understanding)**: Train to reconstruct context
- "Given this expression, what principal insight is being communicated?"
- "What does this expression assume about the audience?"
- "What deficiencies is the expression trying to overcome?"

**Level 3 (Judgment)**: Train to verify interpretations
- "Does this interpretation fit the genetic sequence?"
- "Is this a position or a counterposition?"
- "What is the evidence for this interpretation?"

**Level 4 (Decision)**: Train to adapt for audience
- "How should this be expressed for a different audience?"
- "What transformation of expression is required?"

### The Counterposition in LLM Interpretation

The major counterposition in LLM interpretation:

> "If the real is the 'out there' and knowing it is taking a look, then the ideal of interpretation has to be as close an approximation as possible to a reconstruction of the cinema of what was done, of the soundtrack of what was said, and even of the Huxleyan 'feelie' of the emotions and sentiments."

This counterposition treats interpretation as **reproduction** rather than **understanding**.

The correct position:
> "The interpreter's business is not to create nonexistent evidence but to understand the evidence that exists."

For LLMs, this means:
- Not: Generate text that "sounds like" the original
- But: Grasp the insight being communicated and reformulate for the audience

## System on the Move vs. Static System

> "From the viewpoint of the electronic computer, which coincides with the viewpoint of logic as a technique, system has to be static system. System on the move has to be outlawed."

This is the fundamental limitation of current LLMs:
- They operate as if meaning were static
- But human meaning has its source in **moving system**
- An intelligent writer advances in insight as writing
- An intelligent reader advances in insight as reading

**Implication**: Training for cognitive operations must model system on the move:
- Level 2 (Understanding) should show accumulating insights
- Level 3 (Judgment) should show developing evidence
- The relation between inputs and outputs should not be static mapping but dynamic development

## Conclusion

Lonergan's theory of interpretation provides a complete structure of the inferences required:

1. **Extrapolation** from interpreter's immanent sources to author's meaning
2. **Reconstruction** of context, audience, deficiencies
3. **Verification** through genetic and dialectical coherence
4. **Transposition** to new audience with new context

For LLMs to approximate this:
- Training must embody all four cognitive levels
- The universal viewpoint must be approximated through breadth of training
- Genetic and dialectical sequences must be made explicit
- The distinction between expression and meaning must be maintained

The fundamental challenge is that LLMs lack **self-appropriation**—they have the contents but not the self-knowledge. Training for cognitive operations is an attempt to supply, through operational patterns, what self-appropriation would provide through self-knowledge.
