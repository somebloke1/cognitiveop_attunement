# Cognitive Domain Scheme

A systematic account of how the four levels of cognitive operation differ across five domains of inquiry. Derived from Bernard Lonergan's *Insight*.

## The Four Levels of Cognitive Operation

| Level | Operation | Question | Precept | Output |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|--------|
| 1. Empirical Consciousness | Sensing, perceiving, imagining | (presents data) | BE ATTENTIVE | Presentations |
| 2. Intelligent Consciousness | Inquiry, insight, formulation | What is it? Why? | BE INTELLIGENT | Propositions (hypotheses) |
| 3. Rational Consciousness | Reflection, grasping unconditioned, judgment | Is it so? | BE REASONABLE | Judgment (yes/no/insufficient) |
| 4. Responsible Consciousness | Deliberation, evaluation, decision | Is it good? Worth doing? | BE RESPONSIBLE | Commitment to action |

## The Five Domains

1. **Mathematical** - Formal relations, necessity, proof
2. **Empirical Scientific** - Sensible data, correlation, verification
3. **Common Sense** - Practical particulars, things as related to us
4. **Pop Science / Common Nonsense** - Form without substance, unfulfilled conditions
5. **Philosophic** - Self-appropriation, positions and counterpositions

---

## Level 1: Attention (Empirical Consciousness)

The image is necessary for insight. "No one just wonders. We wonder about something."

### Mathematical Domain
- **Data presented**: Symbols, definitions, axioms, diagrams, previously proven theorems
- **Type of image**: Abstract, schematic (cartwheel becoming circle)
- **Pattern of experience**: Intellectual - detached from practical concerns
- **What is attended to**: Formal elements, relations between symbols

### Empirical Scientific Domain
- **Data presented**: Measurements, observations, instrument readings, experimental results
- **Type of image**: Sensible data, spatiotemporal configurations
- **Pattern of experience**: Intellectual - selective attention guided by heuristic anticipation
- **What is attended to**: Recurrent patterns, correlations, anomalies

### Common Sense Domain
- **Data presented**: Concrete particulars of the situation *as related to me/us*
- **Type of image**: Practical, dramatic, aesthetic - embedded in life
- **Pattern of experience**: Practical/dramatic - governed by concern for self and others
- **What is attended to**: What matters for action, what affects me/us

### Pop Science / Common Nonsense Domain
- **Data presented**: Headlines, summaries, authority claims, social signals
- **Type of image**: Mediated, filtered, pre-interpreted
- **Pattern of experience**: Practical/dramatic - seeking reassurance, belonging
- **What is attended to**: Who said it, how many believe it, social implications

### Philosophic Domain
- **Data presented**: One's own conscious operations, history of philosophy
- **Type of image**: The operations themselves as performed
- **Pattern of experience**: Intellectual - heightened self-awareness
- **What is attended to**: The structure of knowing itself

## Level 2: Understanding (Intelligent Consciousness)

"Insight is the act of catching on to a connection between imagined [elements]."

### The Genesis of Insight (Five Moments)
1. **Awakening** - Release from biological drive, emergence of wonder
2. **The Clue** - Hint, suggestion, "we have got hold of something"
3. **The Process** - Imagination cooperating with intelligent supposition
4. **Achievement** - Question, insight, image, concept present "a solid front"
5. **Expression** - Patterned set of concepts

### Mathematical Domain
- **Form of inquiry**: "What follows from these definitions/axioms?"
- **Type of insight**: Into formal relations, necessity, impossibility
- **How insight occurs**: By supposing ("Let us suppose it has only position")
- **Concepts emerge**: Circle of terms and relations - "terms fix relations, relations fix terms, insight fixes both"
- **Higher viewpoints**: New rules emerge from insight into operations under old rules (arithmetic → algebra)
- **Inverse insight**: Surds, transcendental numbers, noncountable multitudes
- **Output**: Propositions with formal necessity

### Empirical Scientific Domain
- **Form of inquiry**: "What is the law? Why does this occur? How often?"
- **Type of insight**: Classical (systematic correlations) or Statistical (nonsystematic frequencies)
- **How insight occurs**: Into images of data - seeking explanatory correlation
- **Concepts emerge**: Explanatory - relate things to one another (not to us)
- **Higher viewpoints**: More comprehensive theories subsuming earlier ones
- **Inverse insight**: Newton's first law (no intelligibility in constant velocity), special relativity
- **Output**: Hypotheses for verification

### Common Sense Domain
- **Form of inquiry**: "What's going on? How do I handle this?"
- **Type of insight**: Into this particular situation, not universal laws
- **How insight occurs**: Accumulated insights form a "nucleus" with situation-specific additions
- **Concepts emerge**: Descriptive - relate things to us
- **Higher viewpoints**: Not sought - common sense resists theorizing about itself
- **Inverse insight**: Rare - common sense expects intelligibility everywhere
- **Output**: Practical understanding, know-how

### Pop Science / Common Nonsense Domain
- **Form of inquiry**: Truncated - seeks confirmation, not genuine insight
- **Type of "insight"**: Into the FORM of science without the SUBSTANCE
- **How "insight" occurs**: Vocabulary substitutes for understanding
- **Concepts emerge**: Borrowed, not generated from insight
- **Higher viewpoints**: Cannot occur - no genuine lower viewpoint to build on
- **Inverse insight**: Absent - doesn't distinguish levels of intelligibility
- **Output**: Pseudo-propositions that LOOK like hypotheses

### Philosophic Domain
- **Form of inquiry**: "What am I doing when I'm knowing? Why is that knowing?"
- **Type of insight**: Into the structure of cognitional operations themselves
- **How insight occurs**: Self-appropriation - attending to one's own attending, understanding one's own understanding
- **Concepts emerge**: Terms for invariant pattern: experience, understanding, judgment, decision
- **Higher viewpoints**: The critical viewpoint that grounds all others
- **Inverse insight**: The empirical residue, counterpositions as lacking coherence
- **Output**: Positions on knowing, being, objectivity

## Level 3: Judgment (Rational Consciousness)

The virtually unconditioned: (1) a conditioned, (2) a link to conditions, (3) fulfillment of conditions.

### Mathematical Domain
- **Structure of virtually unconditioned**: Conditions fulfilled in intelligibility itself
- **The conditioned**: The theorem/proposition
- **The link**: If these axioms/definitions, then this theorem
- **Fulfillment**: Grasped in the PROOF - internal coherence, no reversion to sense data
- **Judgment types**: Necessary (if axioms, then theorem), Impossible (contradicts axioms)
- **Criterion**: Understanding the proof IS grasping the unconditioned

### Empirical Scientific Domain
- **Structure of virtually unconditioned**: Conditions fulfilled by reversion to sense data
- **The conditioned**: The hypothesis/theory
- **The link**: If hypothesis, then these observable predictions
- **Fulfillment**: Experimental verification - data matches predictions
- **Judgment types**: Verified, Falsified, Probable, Insufficient evidence
- **Criterion**: Canons of empirical method (parsimony, relevance, residues)

### Common Sense Domain
- **Structure of virtually unconditioned**: Conditions fulfilled in practical situation
- **The conditioned**: "This is what's happening / This will work"
- **The link**: Accumulated insights about how things work around here
- **Fulfillment**: Pragmatic - does it work? Did it turn out as expected?
- **Judgment types**: Confident, Doubtful, "Let's try and see"
- **Criterion**: Self-correcting process of learning through collaboration

### Pop Science / Common Nonsense Domain
- **Structure of virtually unconditioned**: CONDITIONS CLAIMED BUT NOT FULFILLED
- **The conditioned**: Pseudo-proposition presented as scientific
- **The link**: Appeals to authority, popularity, scientific vocabulary
- **Fulfillment**: NOT VERIFIED - but presented as if it were
- **Judgment types**: False confidence, premature certainty
- **Detection criteria**:
  - "The question is NOT how many people say it is obvious, nor how great is their authority and renown, but simply what is the evidence"
  - Correlation claimed as causation
  - Vocabulary without understanding
  - Common sense overreaching into explanatory domains

### Philosophic Domain
- **Structure of virtually unconditioned**: Conditions fulfilled in performative self-consistency
- **The conditioned**: Position on knowing, being, objectivity
- **The link**: If grasping intelligently and affirming reasonably, then coherent with that activity
- **Fulfillment**: The act of knowing does not contradict the content known
- **Judgment types**: Position (invites development) or Counterposition (invites reversal)
- **Criterion**: Can this be held coherently by one who grasps it intelligently and affirms it reasonably?

## Level 4: Decision (Responsible Consciousness)

The question: Is it good? Is it worth doing? Deliberation proceeds from judgments of value.

### Mathematical Domain
- **Objects of deliberation**: Which proof strategy? Which axiom system? Which problem to pursue?
- **Values at stake**: Elegance, simplicity, fruitfulness, generality
- **How deliberation proceeds**: Aesthetic and heuristic considerations
- **Decision structure**: Choose approach most likely to yield insight

### Empirical Scientific Domain
- **Objects of deliberation**: Which experiment? Which hypothesis to pursue? Resource allocation?
- **Values at stake**: Truth, utility, technological application, ethical constraints
- **How deliberation proceeds**: Balance of theoretical promise and practical feasibility
- **Decision structure**: Choose investigation most likely to advance understanding

### Common Sense Domain
- **Objects of deliberation**: What to do in this situation? How to handle this?
- **Values at stake**: Practical goods, relationships, immediate and long-term welfare
- **How deliberation proceeds**: Balancing competing goods, consulting others
- **Decision structure**: Choose action that seems best for me/us here and now

### Pop Science / Common Nonsense Domain
- **Objects of deliberation**: Corrupted by false judgments
- **Values at stake**: Social conformity mistaken for truth, feeling informed
- **How deliberation proceeds**: Based on unfounded confidence
- **Decision structure**: Acts on pseudo-knowledge, vulnerable to manipulation

### Philosophic Domain
- **Objects of deliberation**: What kind of knower am I becoming? Am I being authentic?
- **Values at stake**: Intellectual, moral, religious conversion
- **How deliberation proceeds**: Existential - the question of one's fundamental stance
- **Decision structure**: Vertical liberty - choosing one's horizon

## Cross-Cutting Structures

### The Empirical Residue
Positive data denied immanent intelligibility, connected with compensating higher intelligibility:

| Residue | Compensating Intelligibility | Method |
|---------|------------------------------|--------|
| Particular places and times | Universal laws | Scientific collaboration |
| Individual differences | General explanations | Scientific generalization |
| Coincidental aggregates | Probabilities | Statistical method |
| Unintelligent conduct | Dialectical positions/counterpositions | Dialectical method |

### Genetic vs. Dialectical Development
- **Genetic**: System on the move - insights accumulate, understanding develops continuously
- **Dialectical**: Positions vs. counterpositions - either develops toward fuller truth or shifts ground to avoid reversal

### Mystery vs. Myth
- **Mystery**: Proper orientation toward the "known unknown" - what exceeds current understanding
- **Myth**: Counterposition clothed in imagery - "begins in mystery, ends in myth"

### Nominal vs. Explanatory Definition
- **Nominal**: Tells about correct word usage (requires only insight into language)
- **Explanatory**: Includes postulational elements (requires insight into objects)

### Abstraction
"To grasp the essential and disregard the incidental" - the selectivity of intelligence.
- NOT sensible gestalt
- NOT common names
- NOT attending to one question at a time
- IS grasping what is significant and setting aside the irrelevant

## Training Data Implications

For each domain, training samples should demonstrate:

1. **Level 1**: What kind of data/image is attended to
2. **Level 2**: How inquiry proceeds, what form insight takes, how concepts emerge
3. **Level 3**: How conditions are identified, how fulfillment is checked, what warrants judgment
4. **Level 4**: What values are at stake, how deliberation proceeds, what grounds decision

Special attention for **Pop Science / Common Nonsense**:
- Training should show DETECTION of unfulfilled conditions presented as fulfilled
- Contrast with genuine scientific judgment
- Identify markers: authority-appeal, correlation-as-causation, vocabulary-without-understanding
