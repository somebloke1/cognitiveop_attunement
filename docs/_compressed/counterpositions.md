# Counterpositions (Compressed)

## Definition

A **counterposition** is a claim that contradicts the conditions of its own affirmation.

## The Basic Position vs. Counterposition

### Position (coherent with performance)
1. **The Real** = what is intelligently grasped and reasonably affirmed
2. **Knowing** = intelligent inquiry and reasonable affirmation
3. **Objectivity** = consequence of authentic cognitive operation

### Counterposition (incoherent with performance)
1. **The Real** = "already out there now" (biological criterion)
2. **Knowing** = "taking a look" (ocular metaphor)
3. **Objectivity** = extroversion, correlation, consensus

## Why Counterpositions Are Self-Defeating

To affirm a counterposition requires:
1. Grasping it **intelligently**
2. Affirming it **reasonably**

But intelligent grasping and reasonable affirming **presuppose the basic positions**.

**Example**: "Knowledge is nothing but neural activity"
- Affirming this requires intelligent logic, not just neural activity
- If true, the affirmation itself is "nothing but" neural activity
- But neural activity cannot be **true**—it can only be neural activity
- The position undermines itself

## The Dialectical Dynamic

**Positions invite development**: coherent with inquiry → further inquiry possible → fuller positions

**Counterpositions invite reversal**: incoherent with affirmation → tension → either:
- **Reversal**: return to basic positions
- **Shifting ground**: reformulate to avoid reversal (generates new counterposition)

## Detection Criteria

Ask: Does this claim presuppose that:
1. The real is "already out there now"? → Counterposition
2. Knowing is "taking a look"? → Counterposition  
3. Objectivity is correlation or consensus? → Counterposition

Scan for claims that **invalidate the act of claiming**.

## Common Counterpositions in AI/Cognitive Modeling

| Counterposition | Why Self-Defeating |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Intelligence = pattern matching | Identifying this requires more than pattern matching |
| Understanding = statistical correlation | Recognizing correlation ≠ causation requires more than correlation |
| Meaning = vector embeddings | The meaning of "meaning = vectors" cannot itself be a vector |
| Knowledge = neural activity | The truth claim presupposes what it denies |

## The Fundamental Error

**Treating products of intelligence as the operation of intelligence itself**:
- Data is a product of attention (not attention itself)
- Patterns are products of understanding (not understanding itself)
- Verified theories are products of judgment (not judgment itself)

## Evaluation Implications

For detecting counterpositions in student completions:
1. Does reasoning treat data as "already out there now" vs. material for inquiry?
2. Does reasoning conflate Level 2 (hypothesis) with Level 3 (judgment)?
3. Does reasoning substitute authority/consensus for evidence?
4. Does the judgment commit performative contradiction?

**Pop science detection**: Mimics scientific form without verification substance. Uses vocabulary without understanding. Treats correlation as causation. Appeals to authority over evidence.
