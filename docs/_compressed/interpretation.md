# Interpretation and Inference (Compressed)

## The Structure of Expression

Every expression has components:

```
Expression = Verbal flow governed by practical insight F

F depends upon:
  A = Principal insight to be communicated
  B = Grasp of audience's habitual development C
  D = Grasp of deficiencies E to be overcome for A to be communicated
```

Expression is not "outputting meaning" — it is a practical operation accounting for:
1. What one wants to communicate (A)
2. What the audience already knows (C)
3. What the audience lacks (E)

## The Universal Viewpoint

Solution to relativity of interpretation: a **potential totality of genetically and dialectically ordered viewpoints**.

Characteristics:
- **Potential**, not actual (heuristic structure, not complete system)
- **Of viewpoints**, not expressions (principal acts of meaning)
- **Ordered** by: adequate self-knowledge → genetic series → dialectical alternatives

The core of meaning is the **notion of being**: whatever is (or is thought to be) grasped intelligently and affirmed reasonably.

## Levels of Expression

| Level | Source | Intended Response |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Experience alone | Intersubjective reproduction |
| 2 | Artistically ordered experience | Experience + insight |
| 3 | Reflectively tested ordering | Experience + insight + judgment |
| 4 | Addition of will | All three + practical response |

## Canons of Methodical Hermeneutics

### 1. Canon of Relevance
Begin from universal viewpoint. Interpretation must convey differentiation of protean notion of being.

### 2. Canon of Explanation
Differentiation must be **explanatory** (relate contents to one another) not **descriptive** (relate to us).

Three elements:
1. Genetic sequence of accumulated insights
2. Dialectical alternatives (positions/counterpositions)
3. Differentiation of modes of expression

### 3. Canon of Successive Approximations
Cumulative results with principles of criticism:
- Universal viewpoint determines contributor's particular viewpoint
- Extrapolation conditions recognized
- Genetic sequence of expression understood
- Criterion of truth = virtually unconditioned, not authority

### 4. Canon of Parsimony
- **Negative**: Exclude the unverifiable
- **Positive**: Reach intermediate certitudes before complete explanation

### 5. Canon of Residues
Nonsystematic components exist. Key distinction:
- **Genetic**: Writer advances in insight as writing (moving system)
- **Dialectical**: Stress/distortion from counterpositions

**Critical insight**: "From the viewpoint of the electronic computer, system has to be static system. System on the move has to be outlawed." But human meaning has source in **moving system**.

## Mapping to Inference

For interpretation, the inference structure:

| Inference | From → To | Operation |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Context reconstruction | Expression → Author's C, E | Abductive |
| Principal insight | Expression + context → A | Understanding |
| Genetic placement | A → Position in development | Historical |
| Dialectical assessment | A → Position or counterposition | Critical |
| Audience modeling | Response context → C', E' | Practical |

## The Counterposition in Interpretation

**Wrong**: Interpretation as reproduction (cinema of what was done, soundtrack of what was said)

**Right**: Interpretation as understanding — grasp the insight being communicated and reformulate for the audience.

## Evaluation Implications

For evaluating interpretive judgment:
1. Does student distinguish expression from meaning?
2. Does student reconstruct context (what audience was assumed)?
3. Does student place interpretation in genetic/dialectical sequence?
4. Does student adapt for current context without distorting principal insight?

Key failure: Treating interpretation as static mapping (input → output) rather than system on the move (developing understanding).
