# Self-Appropriation (Compressed)

## The Privileged Judgment

**Self-affirmation**: "I am a knower" — the self both affirms and is affirmed.

### Structure (Virtually Unconditioned)

1. **Conditioned**: "I am a knower"
2. **Link**: "I am a knower if I am a unity characterized by sensing, perceiving, inquiring, understanding, reflecting, grasping the unconditioned, and judging"
3. **Fulfillment**: Given in consciousness (the very asking fulfills the conditions)

### Why Privileged

Self-negation is incoherent:
- **"Am I a knower?"** — To ask is to be rationally conscious
- **Answer "Yes"** — Coherent
- **Answer "No"** — Incoherent (how could non-knower ask/answer?)
- **Answer "I don't know"** — Incoherent (knowing you don't know = knowing)

## Consciousness: What It Is

**NOT** an inward look (ocular metaphor applied to consciousness)

**IS** awareness immanent in cognitional acts:
- Seeing is awareness of color (not looking at seeing)
- Understanding is awareness of intelligibility (not looking at understanding)
- The consciousness is not added to the act; it IS a quality of the act

### Three Levels

| Level | Operations | Awareness |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Empirical | Sensing, perceiving, imagining | Mere givenness of acts |
| Intelligent | Inquiry, insight, formulation | Exhibits intelligence in operating |
| Rational | Reflection, grasping unconditioned, judgment | Demands sufficient reason |

## Unity of Consciousness

**On the object side**: Contents cumulate — what is perceived is inquired about is understood is formulated is reflected on is affirmed.

**On the subject side**: The perceiver IS the inquirer IS the understander IS the affirmer. One consciousness that is empirical, intelligent, and rational.

## The Existential Gap

Operations can occur **without being known**:
- One can sense without understanding sensing
- One can understand without understanding understanding
- One can judge without grasping what judgment is

This creates distance between what one does and what one knows about what one does.

### Polymorphism of Consciousness

Consciousness flows in different patterns: biological, aesthetic, dramatic, practical, intellectual, mystical. These alternate, blend, interfere.

The intellectual pattern:
- Is not native (no one is born in it)
- Is not easy (no one reaches it easily)
- Is not permanent (no one remains in it permanently)

When other patterns dominate: "The universe of being seems as unreal as Plato's noetic heaven, and objectivity spontaneously becomes meeting persons and dealing with things 'really out there.'"

## Self-Appropriation as Cure

**Self-appropriation** = attending to one's attending, understanding one's understanding, judging one's judging, deciding about one's deciding.

Effects:
- Closes the existential gap
- Reveals counterpositions as self-defeating
- Provides criterion for distinguishing positions from counterpositions

## Evaluation Implications

For the philosophic/dialectical domain:
- Fulfillment of conditions is in **performative self-consistency**
- Test: Can this position be held coherently by one who grasps it intelligently and affirms it reasonably?
- The student should recognize when affirming a position contradicts the act of affirming

The AI model cannot perform self-affirmation (no consciousness for fulfillment). But it can be trained in the FORM:
- The structure of the virtually unconditioned
- The pattern of condition-fulfillment
- Detection of performative contradiction
