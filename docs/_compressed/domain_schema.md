# Principled Domain Schema (Compressed)

## Primary Differentiator: Mode of Fulfillment

Where is the virtually unconditioned grasped?

| Mode | Fulfillment Found In | Reversion Required? |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Formal** | Intelligibility itself (system coherence) | No—remains in concepts |
| **Empirical** | Sense data via verified correlations | Yes—to measurements |
| **Common Sense** | Pragmatic situation as related to us | Yes—to circumstances |
| **Dialectical** | Performative self-consistency | Yes—to act of affirming |

This is differentiation by **structure of cognitive act**, not subject matter.

## Formal Mode

Conditions fulfilled in intelligibility itself—no sense data needed.

- Proof proceeds from axioms through valid inference
- Fulfillment grasped when proof is seen valid
- Content is necessary given premises

**Pitfalls**: Plausibility ≠ proof; hidden assumptions; incomplete induction

## Empirical Mode

Conditions fulfilled by reversion to sense data.

**Higher Integration Levels** (each makes systematic what lower leaves coincidental):

| Level | Makes Systematic | Examples |
|-------|-----------------|----------|
| Physical | Subatomic regularities | Physics, chemistry |
| Biological | Metabolism, reproduction | Biology, ecology |
| Sensitive | Coordinated behavior | Neuroscience |
| Intelligent | Meaning, culture | Human sciences, history |

**Critical**: Transition between levels requires INSIGHT, not logic. Biology cannot be derived from chemistry.

**Heuristics**:
- Classical: Systematic laws ("always/never")
- Statistical: Ideal frequencies
- Genetic: Developmental operators

## Common Sense Mode

Conditions fulfilled in pragmatic situation as related to us.

**Key characteristics**:
- Incomplete by design—nucleus completed by situation
- Descriptive (relates things to us), not explanatory
- Self-corrects through social collaboration
- Cannot analyze itself abstractly

**Varieties**: Everyday practical, social/interpersonal, professional, methodically-informed

**The Interface Problem**: Much judgment occurs at interface between common sense and methodical intelligence (patient/physician, citizen/science journalism). This is where pop science emerges when interface malfunctions.

**Pitfall (General Bias)**: Extending legitimate concern for concrete into disregard of larger issues and indifference to long-term results.

## Dialectical Mode

Conditions fulfilled in performative self-consistency.

**Test**: Can this be held coherently by one who grasps it intelligently and affirms it reasonably?

**Basic Positions** (coherent → invite development):
- Real = what is intelligently grasped and reasonably affirmed
- Knowing = intelligent inquiry and critical reflection
- Objectivity = consequence of authentic inquiry

**Counterpositions** (incoherent → invite reversal):
- Real = "already out there now"
- Knowing = "taking a look"
- Objectivity = confrontation/correlation

## Pop Science as Counterposition

Pop science is NOT a domain parallel to physics. It is dialectical counterposition—rationalization of general bias using form without substance of scientific judgment.

**Structure**:
- Conditions CLAIMED but NOT FULFILLED
- Scientific vocabulary without understanding
- Authority appeal over evidence

**Detection**: "The question is NOT how many people say it is obvious, nor how great is their authority and renown, but simply what is the evidence."

## Epistemic Context (Dimension, Not Domain)

Cuts across all modes:

**Cooperative**: Speaker's F oriented toward communicating A accurately. Assume good faith.

**Adversarial**: Speaker's F oriented toward producing false belief. Must detect expression ≠ A, reconstruct actual A, identify deceptive strategy.

## Edge Cases (Probabilistic Modifiers)

Not separate domains but complications in any judgment:

| Type | Challenge |
|------|-----------|
| Self-reference | Proposition refers to own affirmation |
| Vagueness | No sharp boundary for predicate |
| Future contingent | Event not yet occurred |
| Counterfactual | No actual data to revert to |
| Category error | Question presupposes wrong categories |

## Evaluation Application

For each student completion, determine:

1. **What mode?** Where should fulfillment be sought?
2. **Mode-appropriate criteria?** Did student apply right type of verification?
3. **Mode conflation?** Did student treat empirical as formal, or common sense as scientific?
4. **Counterposition detection?** For dialectical mode, did student test performative consistency?
5. **Context awareness?** Did student recognize cooperative vs adversarial?
