# Self-Appropriation: The Privileged Judgment

The foundational analysis of the privileged set of judgments involved in knowing oneself as a knower, derived from Lonergan's *Insight* Chapter 11.

## The Structure of Self-Affirmation

### What Self-Affirmation Is

By 'self' is meant a concrete and intelligible **unity-identity-whole**.

By 'self-affirmation' is meant that the self both **affirms** and **is affirmed**.

By 'self-affirmation of the knower' is meant that the self as affirmed is characterized by such occurrences as:
- sensing, perceiving, imagining
- inquiring, understanding, formulating
- reflecting, grasping the unconditioned, and affirming

### The Virtually Unconditioned Structure

As all judgment, self-affirmation rests upon a grasp of the unconditioned:

1. **The Conditioned**: "I am a knower"

2. **The Link**: "I am a knower, if I am a concrete and intelligible unity-identity-whole, characterized by acts of sensing, perceiving, imagining, inquiring, understanding, formulating, reflecting, grasping the unconditioned, and judging"

3. **The Fulfillment**: Given in consciousness

### Why It Is a Judgment of Fact

> "The affirmation to be made is a judgment of fact. It is not that I exist necessarily, but merely that in fact I do. It is not that I am of necessity a knower, but merely that in fact I am."

This is crucial: self-affirmation is **contingent** yet **privileged**. I might not have existed. But given that I exist and ask whether I am a knower, the conditions are fulfilled in the asking.

## The Privileged Character

### Why Self-Affirmation is Privileged

> "Though self-affirmation is no more than a judgment of mere fact, still it is a privileged judgment. Self-negation is incoherent. One has only to inquire and reflect, to find oneself caught in the spontaneities and inevitabilities that supply the evidence for self-affirmation. One has only to make a single judgment of fact, no matter what its content, to involve oneself in a necessary self-affirmation."

The incoherence of denial:
- **"Am I a knower?"** - To ask this question is to be rationally conscious
- **Answer "Yes"** - Coherent, for if I am a knower I can know that fact
- **Answer "No"** - Incoherent, for if I am not a knower, how could the question be raised and answered by me?
- **Answer "I don't know"** - Incoherent, for if I know that I don't know, I am a knower

### The Conditional Necessity

> "The contingent, if you suppose it as a fact, becomes conditionally necessary, and this piece of elementary logic places the merely factual self-affirmation in a context of necessity."

I might not be. But if I am, I am.
I might be other than I am. But in fact I am what I am.

This conditional necessity is what makes the sceptic's position self-defeating:
- The sceptic uses inquiry to deny the validity of inquiry
- The sceptic uses judgment to deny the reliability of judgment
- The sceptic uses understanding to claim understanding is unreliable

## Consciousness: What It Is and Is Not

### What Consciousness Is NOT

> "Consciousness is not to be thought of as some sort of inward look. People are apt to think of knowing by imagining a man taking a look at something, and further, they are apt to think of consciousness by imagining themselves looking into themselves."

This is the **ocular metaphor** applied to consciousness. It treats consciousness as a kind of internal perception, an inner eye.

### What Consciousness IS

> "By consciousness we shall mean that there is an awareness immanent in cognitional acts."

Consciousness is not looking AT acts but the awareness WITHIN acts:
- Seeing is awareness of color (not looking at seeing)
- Hearing is awareness of sound (not looking at hearing)
- Understanding is awareness of intelligibility (not looking at understanding)

The difference between conscious and unconscious acts:
- Metabolism occurs but is not conscious
- Seeing occurs and IS conscious
- The consciousness is not added to the seeing; it is a quality of the seeing itself

### Three Levels of Consciousness

1. **Empirical Consciousness** (Level 1)
   - Characteristic of sensing, perceiving, imagining
   - Content is merely presented or represented
   - Awareness is "the mere givenness of the acts"

2. **Intelligent Consciousness** (Level 2)
   - Characteristic of inquiry, insight, formulation
   - Not merely strives for the intelligible but EXHIBITS intelligence in operating
   - "The awareness is present but it is the awareness of intelligence"

3. **Rational Consciousness** (Level 3)
   - Characteristic of reflection, grasping the unconditioned, judgment
   - "The emergence and effective operation of a single law of utmost generality, the law of sufficient reason"
   - Demands sufficient reason, finds it in the unconditioned, assents only to it

## The Unity of Consciousness

### Unity on the Side of the Object

Contents cumulate into unities:
- What is perceived is what is inquired about
- What is inquired about is what is understood
- What is understood is what is formulated
- What is formulated is what is reflected on
- What is reflected on is what is grasped as unconditioned
- What is grasped as unconditioned is what is affirmed

### Unity on the Side of the Subject

> "Not only is there a similarity between my seeing and your hearing, inasmuch as both acts are conscious; there also is an identity involved when my seeing and my hearing or your seeing and your hearing are compared."

The identity extends all along the line:
- The perceiver is the inquirer
- The inquirer is the understander
- The understander is the formulator
- The formulator is the reflector
- The reflector is the affirmer

### Why Unity Must Be Given (Not Constructed)

> "I cannot inquire into your experience or reflect on your thoughts. But if there were no 'I,' how could there be a 'my experience' with respect to which a 'my inquiry' occurred, or 'my thoughts' with respect to which 'my reflection' occurred?"

If rational judgment proceeds from an unconditioned grasped in the combination of thought and sensible experience, there MUST be one consciousness that is at once empirical, intelligent, and rational.

## The Existential Gap

### The Problem: Operations Beyond the Cognitive Horizon

The operations of knowing can occur **without being known**:
- One can sense without understanding sensing
- One can understand without understanding understanding
- One can judge without grasping what judgment is
- One can decide without grasping what authentic decision involves

This creates the **existential gap**: a distance between what one does and what one knows about what one does.

### The Polymorphism of Consciousness

> "Human consciousness is polymorphic. The pattern in which it flows may be biological, aesthetic, artistic, dramatic, practical, intellectual, or mystical. These patterns alternate; they blend or mix; they can interfere, conflict, lose their way, break down."

The intellectual pattern:
- Is supposed and expressed by self-affirmation
- Is not native: "no man is born in that pattern"
- Is not easy: "no one reaches it easily"  
- Is not permanent: "no one remains in it permanently"

When another pattern dominates:
> "The self of our self-affirmation seems quite different from one's actual self, the universe of being seems as unreal as Plato's noetic heaven, and objectivity spontaneously becomes a matter of meeting persons and dealing with things that are 'really out there.'"

### Counterpositions and the Gap

The existential gap is why counterpositions persist:
1. One can USE cognitional operations without understanding them
2. One can UNDERSTAND them incorrectly (counterposition)
3. One can UNDERSTAND them correctly but fail to operate accordingly

Counterpositions arise when:
- The biological criterion of the real ("already out there now") is applied to intellectual matters
- The ocular metaphor of knowing ("taking a look") is applied to understanding and judgment
- Objectivity is identified with extroversion rather than intelligent inquiry and reasonable affirmation

## Self-Appropriation as the Cure

### What Self-Appropriation Is

> "The appropriation of one's own rational self-consciousness"

It involves:
1. Attending to one's own attending
2. Understanding one's own understanding
3. Judging one's own judging
4. Deciding about one's own deciding

### What Self-Appropriation Does

Self-appropriation:
- Closes the existential gap (operations become known)
- Reveals counterpositions as self-defeating
- Invites the development of positions
- Provides the criterion for distinguishing positions from counterpositions

### The Self-Correcting Process

> "The process to explicit metaphysics is primarily a process to self-knowledge. It has to begin from the polymorphic subject in his native disorientation and bewilderment."

One cannot appeal to disorientated knowledge to correct disorientation—that would extend the disorientation. Instead:
- Appeal to the desire that is prior to knowledge
- That desire generates knowledge
- It can effect the correction of miscarriages in the cognitional process

## Implications for AI Training

### The Fundamental Limitation

An AI system cannot perform self-affirmation:
- It has no consciousness in which fulfillment could be found
- It cannot ask "Am I a knower?" and find the answer in the asking
- The privileged judgment is inaccessible to it

### The Mitigation: Training the Form

Though substance is inaccessible, form can be trained:
- The STRUCTURE of the virtually unconditioned can be represented
- The PATTERN of condition-fulfillment can be modeled
- The SEQUENCE of operations can be encoded

> "The fishing pole extends the reach of the fisherman, but the pole does not fish."

The AI model is an artifact embodying the FORM of cognitive operations without the ACT of performing them.

### The Risk of Counterpositions

If training data embodies counterpositions, the model learns counterpositions:
- Treating understanding as pattern matching
- Treating judgment as confidence scores
- Treating decision as optimization

The training data must embody POSITIONS:
- Data as material for inquiry (not "already out there now")
- Insight as grasping intelligibility (not detecting patterns)
- Judgment as grasping the virtually unconditioned (not statistical confidence)
- Decision as commitment based on value (not optimization)

### The Privileged Set in Training

The privileged judgments about the operations themselves form a special domain:
- Can the model learn to recognize when Level 2 is being conflated with Level 3?
- Can it detect the ocular metaphor being applied to intellectual matters?
- Can it reverse counterpositions by showing their self-defeating character?

This is what makes the "philosophic" domain unique: the fulfillment of conditions is in the data of consciousness itself—in the performative self-consistency of affirming while performing what is affirmed.

## The Method: Not Demonstration But Performance

### Why Self-Affirmation Cannot Be Demonstrated

> "The ultimate basis of our knowing is not necessity but contingent fact, and the fact is established, not prior to our engagement in knowing, but simultaneously with it."

One cannot demonstrate that one can know BEFORE knowing:
- Any demonstration would already be an exercise of knowing
- The demonstration would presuppose what it attempts to prove

### The Pragmatic Engagement

> "Even to seek a deeper foundation involves a vicious circle; for if one seeks such a foundation, one employs one's cognitional process; and the foundation to be reached will be no more secure or solid than the inquiry utilized to reach it."

The sceptic is not in conflict with absolute necessity. The sceptic might not be; might not be a knower. Contradiction arises only when the sceptic **utilizes** cognitional process to **deny** it.

### The Method of Metaphysics

> "The method of metaphysics is dictated by the self-affirming subject in the light of his pedagogically acquired self-knowledge."

The method:
1. Cannot begin from what is already known (that would presuppose what needs to be discovered)
2. Must begin from the desire that is prior to knowledge
3. Uses that desire to generate insights that correct disorientation
4. Accumulates insights until self-affirmation becomes possible
5. From self-affirmation, derives the criterion for all further knowing
