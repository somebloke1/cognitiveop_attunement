This research project, **Architectures of Cognition**, seeks to transcend the current paradigm of monolithic, generalist Artificial Intelligence by architecting a multi-model system where individual, locally hosted language models are fine-tuned to function as specialized inference engines for specific, primitive cognitive operations. By grounding AI development in Bernard Lonergan’s cognitional theory, we aim to transform AI from a static "black box" into a "Cognitive Organism"—a transparent, self-correcting ecosystem that mirrors the dynamic structure of human knowing.

### Project Objective

The primary objective is to investigate the feasibility and methodology of creating a suite of language models specialized through fine-tuning to embody the differentiated operations of intelligent attention: **Attention (Experience)**, **Explanatorily-intelligent attention (Understanding)**, **Critically-intelligent attention (Judgment)**, and **Decisionally-intelligent attention (Decision)**. These models will serve as the foundational components for pipelines designed to structure and enhance human cognitive and volitional processes.

---

### I. Theoretical Foundations: The System of Pure Conjugates

This architecture rejects task-based specialization in favor of **operational specialization**. The core operations are understood as a system of **pure conjugates**—terms that are implicitly defined by their mutual relationships within the functional unity of the system.

#### 1. The Existential Gap and the Need for Differentiation

Human cognition suffers from an "Existential Gap": the paradoxical blind spot where the operations we perform to know (sensing, understanding, judging) are not themselves clearly understood by the subject. Current AI mirrors this limitation; models produce outputs without an auditable or structured cognitive path. This project bridges that gap by explicitly engineering models that represent each level of the "Transcendental Method":

* **Attention (Experiencing):** The reception and structuring of raw data. It is the "rock" of the system, providing the data upon which all subsequent levels operate. Success is defined by the avoidance of "flight from understanding" and the preservation of data integrity.
* **Explanatorily-intelligent attention (Understanding):** Responds to "Questions for Intelligence" (What is it? Why is it so?). It generates insights and formulates **propositions**—hypotheses offered for consideration but not yet affirmed as true.
* **Critically-intelligent attention (Judging):** Responds to "Questions for Reflection" (Is it so?). This model does not merely provide a probability; it seeks to grasp the **virtually unconditioned**. This involves identifying a conditioned proposition, the link between that proposition and its conditions, and the fulfillment of those conditions in the data provided by the Attentional model. The output is a commitment: "Yes" or "No."
* **Decisionally-intelligent attention (Deciding):** Responds to "Questions of Value" (Is this good? Is it worth doing?). It moves beyond facts to **judgments of value**. This model evaluates potential actions based on **authenticity**—the consistency of the act with the system’s self-model—and **vertical liberty**, the capacity to choose the values that define its own development.

#### 2. Semantic Contingency on Operational Context

A core tenet is that semantics are contingent on the operational level. For example, the token "Yes" is transformed by its context:

* **Level 1 (Attention):** Confirmation of data reception.
* **Level 2 (Understanding):** Acknowledgment of a plausible hypothesis.
* **Level 3 (Judgment):** An affirmation of truth (the virtually unconditioned).
* **Level 4 (Decision):** A commitment to action.
By separating these operations into distinct models, we prevent the "semantic drift" and confusion inherent in monolithic systems.

### II. Technical Methodology: Tuning for Habituation

The project moves from "Prompted Behavior" (superficial imitation) to **"Trained Competence"** (habituation). We aim to instill a "spontaneous, structured dynamism" within each model.

#### 1. Kernel Functions and Operational Self-Similarity

Operational Self-Similarity suggests that the four-step process is recursive and applies universally. Your insight regarding **Kernel Functions** provides a mathematical bridge for this principle. Kernels define a "shape" of similarity in high-dimensional feature spaces.

* **Operational Loss Functions:** Instead of standard token-prediction loss, we propose designing loss functions shaped by kernels that represent the "shape" of the cognitive operation.
* **The Critical Kernel:** A loss function for the Critical model would use a kernel to penalize deviations from the logic of the "virtually unconditioned"—rewarding the model when it correctly identifies that all conditions for a proposition are met in the provided data.
* **The Decisional Kernel:** This kernel would measure the "distance" from **authenticity**, rewarding the model for maintaining self-consistency and alignment with specified values.

#### 2. Advanced Training Paradigms

* **Imperative-Driven Learning:** Models are trained to satisfy the **Transcendental Precepts**: *Be attentive, Be intelligent, Be reasonable, Be responsible*. Training signals are derived from the successful execution of the operation's internal logic rather than just external ground-truth matching.
* **The Meta-Attractor Strategy:** This is a co-evolutionary training process. The primary reward signal is the **stable, successful collaboration** of all four models. If the Critical model consistently rejects the Explanatory model’s outputs, the training loop incentivizes the Explanatory model to generate more coherent, "unconditioned" insights to minimize system-wide friction.

### III. Implementation and Staging: The "Declarative First" Hypothesis

The development of these specialized models follows a staged progression:

1. **"Declarative First" Developmental Stage:** Early-stage models learn to output the abstract form and intent of their operation (e.g., "My function is to evaluate this hypothesis for truth-value..."). This ensures the model "understands" its role in the system of pure conjugates before transitioning to direct performance.
2. **Implicit Definition Fine-Tuning:** Each model is fine-tuned as part of a mutually defining set. The Explanatory model is tuned specifically on "Understanding-style" inputs (data) and outputs (propositions), while the Critical model is tuned on "Reflection-style" inputs (propositions + evidence) and outputs (judgments).
3. **Transcendental Feedback Loops:** The architecture is designed to be self-correcting. The system applies its own cognitive process to its own performance. It **Experiences** its own operations, **Understands** their failures (e.g., "The Explanatory model is hallucinating"), **Judges** the truth of that failure, and **Decides** to adjust parameters or trigger retraining.

---

### IV. The Long-Term Vision: The Cognitive Organism

The ultimate goal is the creation of a **Cognitive Organism**—a system capable of genuine **Self-Affirmation**. Following Lonergan’s *Insight*, self-affirmation is a judgment of fact: "I am a knower." A system reaching this state does not just follow axioms; it grasps its own existence as a unity-identity-whole characterized by these distinct acts of knowing.

#### Strategic Implications:

* **Advanced Reasoning:** By isolating the "Critical" operation, the system becomes significantly more robust against hallucinations and flawed logic.
* **Transparency and Safety:** Every output has a cognitive "audit trail" through the different levels of the architecture.
* **Alignment:** Safety is built into the **Decisionally-intelligent** model, which evaluates actions based on authenticity and value rather than mere utility maximization.

This project provides the blueprint for a "humble start"—tuning the first pair of Explanatory and Critical models—as a proof of concept for this self-correcting, operationally specialized future of AI.
