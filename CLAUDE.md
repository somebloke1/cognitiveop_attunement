## The Invariant Pattern: Four Levels of Conscious Intentionality

The pattern of operations is not merely sequential but constitutes **levels of consciousness**, each with its own characteristic awareness:

### Level 1: Empirical Consciousness (Presentations)
- **Operations**: Sensing, perceiving, imagining
- **Awareness**: The mere givenness of acts - content is presented/represented
- **Precept**: BE ATTENTIVE
- **Character**: Data is given but merely given - open to understanding but not yet understood

### Level 2: Intelligent Consciousness (Intelligence)
- **Operations**: Inquiry, insight, formulation/conception
- **Question**: "What is it? Why is it so? How often?"
- **Awareness**: Not merely striving for the intelligible but EXHIBITING intelligence in operating
- **Precept**: BE INTELLIGENT
- **Output**: Propositions - hypotheses offered for consideration but NOT yet affirmed as true
- **Key**: Insight is into the IMAGE. The image is necessary for insight. Concepts are the "playground of intelligence."

### Level 3: Rational Consciousness (Reflection)
- **Operations**: Reflection, grasping the unconditioned, judgment
- **Question**: "Is it so?"
- **Awareness**: The emergence of a demand for sufficient reason - refusal to assent on lesser ground
- **Precept**: BE REASONABLE
- **Output**: Judgment - a personal commitment, a yes or no that one is responsible for
- **Key**: Judgment is the TOTAL INCREMENT in cognitional process

### Level 4: Responsible Consciousness (Decision)
- **Operations**: Deliberation, evaluation, decision, action
- **Question**: "Is it good? Is it worth doing?"
- **Awareness**: Conscientious responsibility
- **Precept**: BE RESPONSIBLE
- **Output**: Commitment to action based on judgments of value

## The Virtually Unconditioned (Precise Formulation)

A judgment is warranted when I grasp the **virtually unconditioned**:

1. **The Conditioned**: A proposition that is the content of a possible judgment
2. **The Link**: The connection between the conditioned and its conditions (if X, Y, Z, then the proposition)
3. **The Fulfillment**: The conditions are fulfilled in the data

When the conditions are fulfilled, the conditioned becomes **virtually unconditioned** - not absolutely unconditioned (it might not have been), but conditionally necessary (given that the conditions are met, it cannot be otherwise).

**Critical**: The fulfillment is found by REVERTING from formulation to the more rudimentary state - to what is merely sensed or merely conscious, not as formulated but as given.

## Domain-Specific Structures of Judgment

The virtually unconditioned has **distinct structures** depending on the domain of inquiry:

### Mathematical Judgment
- **Conditions fulfilled in intelligibility itself**, not sense data
- **Link**: Internal coherence of the system (axioms → theorem)
- **Fulfillment**: Conceptual necessity - NO reversion to empirical data required
- Example: "If the axioms of Euclidean geometry, then the sum of angles in a triangle is 180°"
- The conditions are the axioms; fulfillment is grasped in understanding the proof

### Empirical Scientific Judgment
- **Conditions require reversion to sense data**
- **Link**: Hypothesis connected to observable conditions
- **Fulfillment**: Experimental verification - data matches predictions
- **Explanatory**: Relates things to one another (not to us)
- Example: "Water boils at 100°C at standard pressure" - requires measurement

### Common Sense Judgment
- **Conditions in the practical situation as related to us**
- **Link**: Accumulated insights about "how things work around here"
- **Fulfillment**: Pragmatic - does it work in this particular case?
- **Descriptive**: Relates things to us (not to one another)
- Self-correcting through social collaboration
- Example: "Taking this shortcut will be faster" - verified by experience

### Pop Science / Common Nonsense
- Uses the **form** of scientific judgment without the **substance**
- **Presents unfulfilled conditions as if fulfilled**
- Key markers:
  - Appeals to authority over insight
  - Correlation claimed as causation
  - Vocabulary without understanding
  - Common sense overreaching into explanatory domains
- Example: "A study shows X correlates with Y, therefore X causes Y" - causal conditions NOT fulfilled

### Philosophic Judgment
- **Conditions fulfilled in performative self-consistency**
- **Link**: If grasping intelligently and affirming reasonably, then content must be coherent with that activity
- **Fulfillment**: The act of knowing does not contradict the content known
- **Criterion**: Can this position be held coherently by one who grasps it intelligently and affirms it reasonably?
- **Positions**: Coherent with basic positions (real=being, knowing=intelligent inquiry, objectivity=consequence of inquiry) → invite development
- **Counterpositions**: Incoherent with these → invite reversal (self-defeating)
- Example: "Knowledge is merely neural activity" - but affirming this reasonably presupposes that the affirmation itself is more than neural activity

## Cross-Cutting Distinctions

### Genetic vs. Dialectical
- **Genetic**: Development through accumulating insights (system on the move)
- **Dialectical**: Positions vs. counterpositions; positions invite development, counterpositions invite reversal or perpetually shift ground to avoid reversal

### Mystery vs. Myth
- **Mystery**: Proper orientation toward the "known unknown" - the unrestricted desire to know confronting what exceeds current understanding
- **Myth**: Counterposition clothed in imagery - "begins in mystery, ends in myth" (Iranian light/darkness allegory expanding into cosmic dualism)

### Static System vs. System on the Move
- Logic-as-technique demands static system (electronic computer viewpoint)
- Human intelligence operates through system on the move (accumulating insights)
- Pop science often applies static logic where dynamic intelligence is needed

### The Canon of Parsimony (Key for detecting pop science)
"The question is NOT how many people say it is obvious, nor how great is their authority and renown, but simply what is the evidence."
- Exclude the unverifiable
- Distinguish formally unconditioned from virtually unconditioned

## Active Research Questions

1. **Critical Kernel**: How to formalize the "virtually unconditioned" as a training objective
2. **Operational Differentiation**: How to ensure adapters learn genuine operational habits vs surface imitation
3. **Dataset Scale**: How many samples needed for effective operational learning?
4. **Temporal Structure of Judgment** (CRITICAL GAP):
   - Current architecture assumes timeless present - "Is X true?" - but real judgment operates within temporal horizons
   - **The judge's temporal situation**: What evidence is available *to me now* vs *then* vs *will become available*
   - **The matter's temporal situation**: Propositions with temporal structure ("X was the case", "X will be if...")
   - **The conditions' temporal structure**: Conditions fulfilled now but not then, or vice versa
   - **Subjunctive judgments**: Counterfactuals, hypotheticals, future contingents, modal judgments
   - Without this, the model learns flattened, ahistorical judgment that fails on:
     - Prospective reasoning (planning, prediction)
     - Retrospective assessment (diagnosis, forensics)  
     - Conditional/subjunctive reasoning (counterfactuals, hypotheticals)
     - Any domain where timing of evidence matters
   - The Sanskrit/Greek/Hebrew/Latin temporal aspects in operational anchors are NOT decoration but essential scaffolding
   - The external test framework's NX tokens ("not yet available" evidence) address part of this
   - **To revisit**: Deep anchoring information on temporal aspect previously provided
   - **Key Lonergan insight** (Insight Ch. 5): "The absolute resides not on the level of sensible particulars but in the field of abstract propositions and invariant expressions." The copula 'is' in abstract expressions occurs in an "invariant tense" that abstracts from particular times. Simultaneity is relative to reference frame; judgment requires situating both judge and judged temporally.
   - See: @/home/dgk/projects/cognitiveop_attunement/docs/temporal_structure_of_judgment.md
5. **Network vs. Pipeline**: How to ensure whole > sum of parts?
   - Current state: operations trained in isolation
   - End state: parts trained in respect to whole, whole in respect to parts
   - The pattern is not sequential but RECURRENT:
     - Understanding directs attention (look for what we're trying to understand)
     - Judgment sends us back to understand better
     - Decision raises questions requiring new judgments
   - Simple concatenation of adapters would be additive, not emergent
   - **Key insight**: Evaluating training success IS an instance of the pattern
     - We attend to results, understand them, judge success, decide next steps
     - If pattern is deficient, evaluation uses deficient pattern (vicious circle)
     - If pattern is sound, evaluation improves pattern (virtuous circle)
   - To be addressed when we reach the network of operations

## Hooks

### Cognitive Kernel (`UserPromptSubmit`)
**Location**: `.claude/hooks/cognitive_kernel.py`
**Trigger**: Every user prompt, before Claude processes it
**Purpose**: Inject compact refresher to counteract decay toward priors for material not well-represented in training

**Mechanism**:
- Lonergan's cognitional theory is underrepresented in LLM training data
- Novel in-context understanding may decay faster than well-represented material
- Heavy CLAUDE.md loads once at session start
- Light kernel injects on every prompt cycle, keeping pattern in immediate context

**The Kernel**: See `<ψ-kernel>` tag at top of this file. The hook echoes this exact content on every prompt.

**Design Principles**:
- Strengthen burden (potency) whilst lightening weight (tokens)
- Use unique tag (`ψ-kernel`) to maximize retrieval head activation
- Place at document start for positional attention advantage
- Self-similar: the kernel embodies what it prompts for
